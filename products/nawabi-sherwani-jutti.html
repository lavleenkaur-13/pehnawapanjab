About this Footwear

The Nawabi Sherwani Jutti is the perfect crown for any groom's ensemble — a handcrafted piece that combines royal Nawabi aesthetics with the durability of traditional Punjabi jutti-making. Each pair is hand-stitched by master karigars in Punjab using techniques passed down through generations.

The pointed toe, signature curved silhouette, and rich brocade overlay make this men's jutti instantly recognizable as a piece of premium heritage footwear. The silk thread embroidery features classic motifs inspired by Mughal-era designs, while the soft leather lining ensures comfort even through long wedding ceremonies.

Pair this Nawabi jutti with a classic sherwani for weddings, or dress it down with a kurta-pyjama for sangeet and festive functions. Each Nawabi Sherwani Jutti is made to order, ensuring authenticity and a perfect fit.
