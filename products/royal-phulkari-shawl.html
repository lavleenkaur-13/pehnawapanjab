About this Accessories

The Royal Phulkari Shawl honours one of Punjab's most cherished textile traditions — phulkari, literally "flower work" — an embroidery craft practiced for over 500 years by Punjabi women. Each shawl in our collection is hand-embroidered by skilled artisans, with stitches so fine that the reverse side is nearly as beautiful as the front.

Our Royal Phulkari Shawl features the classic darning stitch in vibrant silk threads on a fine wool blend base. The floral and geometric motifs are arranged in traditional patterns — some inspired by the famous "bagh" style where the embroidery covers the fabric completely. Every shawl takes between 2 to 4 weeks of dedicated hand-work to complete.

This phulkari shawl is more than an accessory — it is a wearable heirloom. Traditionally given to Punjabi brides as part of their trousseau, a phulkari shawl is meant to be cherished, worn on special occasions, and eventually passed on as a family treasure. Perfect for winter weddings, festive evenings, or as a meaningful gift.
