About this Jewellery

The Heritage Kundan Earrings are designed for the woman who appreciates the artistry of traditional Indian jewellery but wants pieces that fit seamlessly into modern life. These earrings combine the timeless beauty of kundan stone-setting with a lightweight design that's comfortable to wear all day.

Each pair is handcrafted by jewellery artisans who specialize in the kundan technique — where uncut stones are carefully embedded in gold-toned settings using natural lac. The result is a deep, luminous look that machine-made jewellery simply cannot replicate. Pearl drops add a touch of softness, balancing the boldness of the kundan work.

These kundan earrings pair beautifully with everything from a simple cotton kurta for daytime wear to a heavily embroidered lehenga for evening weddings. A versatile, must-have addition to any Punjabi jewellery collection.
