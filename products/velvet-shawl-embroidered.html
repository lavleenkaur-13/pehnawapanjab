About this Accessories

The Embroidered Velvet Shawl is a statement piece of winter heritage fashion — combining the rich texture of premium velvet with the artistry of hand-embroidery techniques perfected over generations in Punjab. Each shawl is crafted as an individual work of art, designed to elevate any winter outfit it accompanies.

Our velvet base is sourced for its deep pile and lustrous finish — a material that has always been associated with luxury and ceremony in Indian textile tradition. The embroidery features a careful combination of metallic zari for shine, dori thread for raised texture, and silk resham for the floral motifs. Every shawl takes between 3 to 6 weeks of hand-work, with multiple artisans contributing their specialized skills.

This embroidered velvet shawl is your perfect companion for winter weddings, formal evenings, and ceremonial functions. Drape it over a silk suit for instant elegance, wear it with a lehenga for outdoor winter weddings, or simply use it to add warmth and beauty to any ensemble. A piece that is genuinely meant to be treasured for decades.
