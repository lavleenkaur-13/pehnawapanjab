About this Apparel

The Royal Wedding Lehenga is the ultimate Punjabi bridal statement — a meticulously handcrafted three-piece ensemble that has been envisioned for the most important day of your life. Designed with the discerning Punjabi bride in mind, this lehenga combines traditional motifs with contemporary silhouettes for a look that feels both timeless and modern.

The lehenga skirt features a 6-meter flared cut crafted from raw silk, embellished with intricate hand-embroidery including zari work, sequin detailing, semi-precious stone accents, and traditional resham thread embroidery. The fitted choli is similarly embellished, while the net dupatta is finished with a heavy border and scattered embroidery throughout — designed to drape gracefully as either a head veil or shoulder piece.

Every Royal Wedding Lehenga is made to order, with careful attention to fit, silhouette and bridal preference. The embroidery alone takes between 45 to 90 days of skilled hand-work involving multiple master karigars. This is not a lehenga you simply wear — it is a heirloom you create memories in. Pair with our Amritsari Kundan Set for the complete bridal look.
